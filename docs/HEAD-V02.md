# v0.2 replacement head — print and assembly

![Revised head](images/head-v02.png)

Keep the existing handle. Print one each of `nose-a.stl`, `nose-b.stl`, `jaw-a.stl`, `jaw-b.stl`, and `fixed-jaw.stl`, or print the combined `head-layout.stl`. These five parts replace the original two nose halves and one-piece jaw.

The new head is **43 × 18.34 × 9.4 mm** closed, excluding hardware protrusions. Its printed material volume is about **39.5% lower** than v0.1. The front cheek plates stop behind the gripping face. The 4 mm moving and 4.6 mm fixed contact tips trade a little contact width for more robust filament printing.

The two cheeks now include **2.4 mm reinforcing webs** filling the rear triangular windows. The rod passage and rounded linkage access remain open. Blue and teal identify the cheeks, coral and yellow the moving jaw halves, and magenta the fixed insert. Preview colors are for identification; use a single PLA filament for the supplied slice.

## Print

The supplied [A1 print package](../prints/fox-build-a1-head-v02/PRINT-GUIDE.md) estimates **33 minutes 32 seconds and 4.49 g of PLA**, including brims. It uses a 0.4 mm nozzle, textured PEI plate, 0.16 mm layers, 4 walls, 50% gyroid, and 2 mm outside brims. **Supports are off.** Each part's continuous broad face starts on the bed. Keep the supplied orientations: grooves and shallow pockets face upward.

![Five pieces laid flat](images/head-v02-layout.png)

After printing, remove brims and clean mating edges. The 4.0 mm moving jaw fits a 4.6 mm fork, leaving 0.3 mm clearance per side. Clean the 3.25 mm pivot and 2.25 mm link/mounting holes gently until fasteners slide through. Remove any stringing from the 1.5 mm central rod slot.

## Hardware and assembly

Reuse the 116 mm tube, 1 mm pushrod with eyes 140 mm apart, three M3 × 20 mm head fasteners (one pivot and two tube-clamp screws), and one M2 linkage screw. Add **two M2 × 12 mm screws, two M2 nuts, and four thin M2 washers** for the fixed-tip insert. These are additional pieces of the same sizes in the existing assortment. Use washers no more than 5 mm OD on the insert. The 9.4 mm stack plus thin washers and nut fits a 12 mm screw nominally; verify full nut engagement, or use longer M2 screws if needed.

1. Face the shallow pockets of Jaw A and Jaw B inward and put the rod eye into their combined slot. Install the M2 linkage screw through the two halves and the eye, leaving the eye free to turn.
2. Put the fixed insert between Nose A and Nose B, with its flat contact face toward the moving jaw. Its two staggered holes match the small holes along the cheeks' lower arms. Secure those two M2 mounting screws.
3. Place the split moving jaw between the cheeks and pass the M3 pivot through both cheeks and both jaw halves. Tighten only enough to retain it while allowing free rotation.
4. Fit the original tube into the two half-round seats and install the two M3 clamp screws loosely. With the jaw closed, align the head and handle pivot centers 140 mm apart and in the same plane. Each tube end remains 12 mm inward from its pivot. The new head overlaps about 13 mm of the tube; no recut is needed.
5. Shim the tube sockets as necessary, secure gently, and check closed-tip contact. Cycle at least 20 times before gripping a paper strip. Watch for binding, flexing of the split jaw, and lateral tip mismatch.

The handle-to-head motion and rod-eye geometry are unchanged. The smaller head is not yet physically validated. Its 1.25 mm thick crank cheeks beside the central wire slot deserve particular inspection during the first gentle fit test.
